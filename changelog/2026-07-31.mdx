The latest user interface and R5 release (**v7.6**) from the Conveyal team include:
- New ways of representing and importing [on-demand services](/learn-more/demand-responsive)
- Improved transit alignment editing. When editing dwell times and speeds, associated stops/segments are highlighted more clearly on the map. And segment-specific speed or travel time values are now recorded more accurately; previous versions of Conveyal truncated any segment-specific travel times, which may have led transit travel times to be lower than intended.
- Options to display the baseline network on maps of regional analysis results
- More resilient regional analysis progress, with regular checks for unexpected shutdown of cloud workers
- GeoJSON download of additional modification types
- Additional detail in [transit path CSV outputs](/guides/compute-freeform#path-details)
- Updated LODES job and workforce data for regions in the United States
